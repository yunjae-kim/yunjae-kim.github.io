High School Student Researcher | North Vancouver, BC

I am interested in how lattice and electronic structure give rise to emergent thermal properties and transport phenomena in solid-state systems and their applications to materials science and engineering. I have conducted research in predicting high-temperature material properties using theoretical tools, experimentally analyzing cryogenic MOSFET behaviors, and designing novel dye molecules and analyzing them using DFT.
